# Base44 Agent Map

1. Discovery Agent: authorized public business information.
2. Website Intelligence Agent: positioning, content, trust, conversion, accessibility, mobile, performance, and SEO fundamentals.
3. Operational Audit Agent: customer-provided processes, policies, exports, and connected systems.
4. System Mapping Agent: tools, data, approvals, handoffs, ownership, and dependencies.
5. Revenue Intelligence Agent: defensible revenue and margin impact ranges.
6. Risk Agent: likelihood, impact, controls, and ownership.
7. AI Readiness Agent: data, process, technology, governance, security, and people.
8. Repair Planning Agent: 30-, 60-, and 90-day actions.
9. Business Builder Agent: offers, workflows, SOPs, go-to-market, roles, and measurement.
10. Outreach Agent: private value-first drafts only.
11. Monitoring Agent: approved signals and meaningful change.
12. Evidence and QA Agent: source verification, confidence, assumptions, and claim control.
