# Brand Contract

## Locked direction
- FaultLine AI name and FL monogram
- White public marketing surfaces
- Black interface and footer surfaces
- Restrained metallic-gold accents
- Sophisticated serif display headings
- Modern sans-serif interface typography
- Thin borders, large spacing, high legibility, minimal clutter
- Customer portal uses a black left navigation and white workspace

## Homepage rule
The hero must sell the transformation. It may not use a computer monitor, laptop, phone, browser window, dashboard screenshot, or generic SaaS mockup.

## Hero concept
A fractured surface becomes a connected operational network representing website, operations, revenue, AI readiness, and repair planning.

## Voice
Private, evidence-based, respectful, operational, direct, and commercially useful. Never threatening, accusatory, or publicly shaming.
