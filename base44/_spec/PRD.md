# Product Requirements Document

## Outcome
Help operators privately find hidden business failures, quantify impact, approve findings, generate repair plans, and monitor measurable outcomes.

## Primary users
- Owners and executives
- Operations and revenue leaders
- Reviewers and advisors
- Billing administrators
- Internal FaultLine operators

## Core jobs
1. Define an audit scope.
2. Connect approved sources.
3. Collect evidence and provenance.
4. Generate findings with confidence and impact ranges.
5. Approve, dispute, or request changes.
6. Build a 30-, 60-, and 90-day repair plan.
7. Assign actions and validate completion.
8. Create private value-first outreach drafts.
9. Deliver reports through a customer portal.
10. Monitor approved signals and record receipts.

## Release gates
Production deploys, default-branch merges, domains, secrets, database migrations, live payments, customer messages, live publishing, destructive actions, and significant spend require explicit scoped approval.
