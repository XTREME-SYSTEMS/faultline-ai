# Base44 RLS Matrix

- Organization: members read; owner/admin update.
- Membership: users read their workspace; owner/admin manage.
- Companies, websites, audits, evidence, findings, risks, revenue leaks, system maps, repair plans, actions, reports, and monitoring: organization members only.
- Approvals: reviewer, admin, and owner decisions.
- Outreach: members may draft; only an approved server workflow may send.
- Workflow runs, receipts, dead letter, and audit logs: authorized read; server/admin mutation.
